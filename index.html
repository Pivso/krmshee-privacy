Krmshee — Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026. Applies to the Krmshee app on Google Play (io.github.pivso.krmshee).

Krmshee is a card game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers, and it does not collect personal data. This page says exactly what the app keeps and where anything goes.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings: sound and vibration, the name you play under, the figure and colour you picked, and the drink you chose.
  • A random table ticket the app makes up so that a phone that drops out of a multiplayer game can take its seat back. It identifies nothing but that seat.
  • A solo game in progress, so it can be resumed if the app is closed.

None of this is sent anywhere by Krmshee. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

If you have Android's own backup switched on, Android may include these settings in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is made and kept by Google under your account; Krmshee has no access to it.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer runs on a Wi-Fi hotspot that one of the players turns on. The phones talk to each other directly over that hotspot — never over the internet, and never through any server.

What travels between the phones is what a game needs: each player's name, figure and colour, drink choice, and the moves of the game. It is not encrypted beyond the hotspot's own Wi-Fi password, so someone else connected to the same hotspot could in principle see it. It contains nothing but names and card moves, and it is gone when the game ends.

Sharing a game log

The leave-the-table screen has a button that shares a log of the current round — its cards, moves and seat names. Nothing is sent unless you press it, and it goes only to the app or person you choose in Android's share sheet.

Permissions

  • Internet / local network access: needed to open the connections between phones on the hotspot. The app does not contact anything on the internet.
  • Network state: used to find the hotspot the phone is on.

Krmshee does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Krmshee is a drinking game meant for adults and is not directed at children.
